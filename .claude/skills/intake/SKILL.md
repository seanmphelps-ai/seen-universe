# Intake

## Purpose
Seal clean subject and location-period inputs before downstream calculation.

## Required
- name when requested by the active flow
- birth date
- birth location
- every lived location of 6 months or more
- current location
- repeated stays as separate periods
- trip/event location and dates when relevant
- confirmed geographic record and provenance
- calendar timeframe for every location-period
- time certainty stored separately

## Rules
- Earth before Sky.
- Do not invent missing dates, places, coordinates, or time certainty.
- Preserve approximate dates as approximate.
- Do not begin birth-time rectification before date and location material exists.
- Do not modify calculation, rendering, or generator architecture from this skill.

## Output
Validated structured intake records suitable for persistence and downstream skills.
