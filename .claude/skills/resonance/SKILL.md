# Resonance

## Purpose
Capture recognition and calibration evidence from the subject without rewriting source calculations.

## Rules
- Present bounded candidate material derived from preserved calculations and evidence.
- Store the subject's selections as calibration evidence.
- Never change a given Gate, Line, planetary position, source extraction, environmental record, or other original calculation because a candidate resonates or fails to resonate.
- Preserve uncertainty and contradiction.
- Keep user recognition separate from source truth.
- Birth-time rectification may use resonance only within the approved rectification protocol.

## Output
Versioned resonance/calibration records linked to the candidates and source records that produced them.
