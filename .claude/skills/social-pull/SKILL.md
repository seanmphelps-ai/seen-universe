# Social Pull

## Purpose
Collect timeframe-locked public observable evidence for one location-period at a time.

## Evidence universe
Use provider-agnostic adapters across relevant public sources such as social platforms, forums, reviews, local web/news, events, commerce, jobs, housing, search, and movement/place signals. Official data is baseline, history, corroboration, and validation rather than the primary social engine.

## Preserve
- source and provider
- observation timestamp
- subject timeframe
- requested geography
- matched geography
- geographic precision
- raw evidence reference
- unique event / post / account identity where available

## Rules
- Do not project current evidence backward into a historical stay.
- Do not treat provider popularity as representativeness.
- Deduplicate the underlying event from its social spread.
- Preserve absence and contradiction.
- Do not interpret personality in this skill.

## Output
Evidence records ready for `narrative-extract`.
