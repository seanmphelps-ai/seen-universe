## Phase 4 — Regulation Layer

Regulation appears after the dark extraction for that unit, but this is not the full healing/capacity system.

This layer exists to keep the user in contact with the truth without flooding, bypassing, or collapsing.

This is mandatory. The dark chart is not shipped without it.

Each section closes with:

- recognition without shame
- somatic cue
- breathwork or grounding
- one small daily action
- cadence tracker link
- return to agency

The final close must include:

- what to do in the next 10 minutes
- what to track for the next 24 hours
- what pattern to watch for this week
- when to use the Voice Oracle
- how to enter Cadence instead of spiraling
- a reminder that recognition is not a sentence; it is a starting point

Suggested output seal:

- Truth
- Translation
- Return

Do not use this layer to prematurely “heal” the wound, invert the chart, or render the thriving self. That belongs in the separate capacity chart.

### Post-Extraction Product Layer — Cadence Tracking
