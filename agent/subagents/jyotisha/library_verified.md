## #7 Porutham (South Indian marriage matching): KP ch. XIII "To determine the suitability of a match" (chapter heading p.68)

**7.1 The ten considerations** (KP XIII p.69)
- They are Dhinam, Ganam, Mahendhram, Sthree-Dheergham, Yoni, Rasi, Rasyadhipathi, Vasyam, Rajju and Vedhai.

**7.2 Dhinam** (KP XIII p.69)
- Count from the bride's star to the groom's star, both inclusive. Read the count in nines: 1 Janma, 2 Sampath (fortune), 3 Vipath (disaster), 4 Kshema (well-being), 5 Prathyara (obstruction), 6 Sadhaka (aid), 7 Vadham (slaughter), 8 Maithra (friendship), 9 Parama-Maithra (intimate friendship).
- Agreement: the groom's star is 2nd, 4th, 6th or 8th from the bride's. A 3rd, 5th or 7th count is disagreement.
- Exception (Pariyaya): of the second round, the 1st quarter of the 3rd star, the 4th quarter of the 5th, and the 3rd quarter of the 7th are to be avoided. The other quarters of those stars are acceptable, and the third round produces no evil.
- The groom's star should not be the 27th from the bride's. The harm is reduced if both stars are in the same sign, and it is bad if they are in different signs.
- Also avoid the 22nd star counted from either person's Janma nakshatra (Vadha-Vainasika).

**7.3 Fixed unsuitable and favourable star pairs** (KP XIII p.70)
- Unhappy pairs: Krittika–Aslesha, Aslesha–Swathi, Chithra–Purvasadha, Anuradha–Sravishta, Sravishta–Bharani, Sathabis–Krittika.
- Happy pairs: Ardhra–Utharapalguni, Purvapalguni–Anuradha, Chithra–Pushya, Punarvasu–Hastha. The last pair favours the family's progress.
- Ruinous: Jyeshta and Sathabis; Utharapalguni with Mrigasirsha; Hastha with Mula (death and money loss).
- The page also lists specific groom/bride star pairs with specific results (for example Rohini–Magha: many sons; Aswini–Sravana: enemies). Use the page for the full list.
- Footnote: some of these pairs are exceptions to the Dhinam rule.

**7.4 Common Janma nakshatra** (KP XIII pp.71–72)
- If both share the same star:
  - Excellent: Rohini, Ardhra, Magha, Hastha, Visakha, Sravana, Utharabhadrapada, Revathi.
  - Neutral: Aswini, Krithika, Mrigasirsha, Punarvasu, Pushya, Purvapalguni, Utharapalguni, Chithra, Anuradha, Purvashada, Utharashada.
  - Avoid: Bharani, Aslesha, Swathi, Jyeshta, Mula, Sravishta, Sathabis, Purvabhadrapada.
- If the shared star spans two signs, the groom's quarter must be the earlier one, or it harms the couple. If both are in the same sign, the bride's star must precede the groom's.
- Exception: Bharani–Krittika, Sravishta–Sathabis and Pushya–Aslesha are left out of this consideration when they fall in one sign.
- The groom's star may be the next star after the bride's when hers is Aswini, Krittika, Mrigasirsha, Magha, Hastha, Swathi, Purvashada or Sathabis.
- If the shared star belongs to two signs, the first sign should be the bride's. If the quarters split equally between the signs, the first two quarters' sign should be the groom's.

**7.5 Ganam** (KP XIII p.72)
- Deva: Aswini, Mrigasirsha, Punarvasu, Pushya, Hastha, Swathi, Anuradha, Sravana, Revathi.
- Manushya: Bharani, Rohini, Ardhra, Purvapalguni, Utharapalguni, Purvashada, Utharashada, Purvabhadrapada, Utharabhadrapada. The page prints these as "Utharabhadra padha"; verify it on the image.
- Rakshasa: Krithika, Aslesha, Magha, Chithra, Visakha, Jyeshta, Mula, Sravishta, Sathabis.
- Results:
  - Same gana: excellent.
  - Groom Deva with bride Manushya: middling.
  - Deva with Rakshasa: very bad.
  - Manushya with Rakshasa: fatal.
  - Groom Manushya with bride Rakshasa: harmful to both.
- Exception: Rakshasa-gana harm lessens if the bride's star is beyond the 14th from the groom's.

**7.6 Mahendhram** (KP XIII p.72)
- Good if the groom's star is the 4th, 7th, 10th, 13th, 16th, 19th, 22nd or 25th from the bride's. This promotes well-being and long life.

**7.7 Sthree-Dheergham** (KP XIII p.72)
- The groom's star should be beyond the 13th from the bride's. Some writers accept beyond the 7th.

**7.8 Yoni** (KP XIII p.73)
- Star-animal assignments:
  - Horse: Aswini, Sathabis.
  - Elephant: Bharani, Revathi.
  - Sheep: Pushya, Krithika.
  - Serpent: Rohini, Mrigasirsha.
  - Cat: Aslesha, Punarvasu.
  - Rat: Magha, Purvapalguni.
  - Cow: Utharapalguni, Utharashada, Utharabhadrapada.
  - Buffalo: Swathi, Hastha.
  - Tiger: Visakha, Chithra.
  - Deer: Jyeshta, Anuradha.
  - Dog: Mula, Ardhra.
  - Monkey: Purvashada, Sravana.
  - Lion: Sravishta, Purvabhadrapada.
- Hostile pairs (inauspicious): Monkey–Sheep, Deer–Elephant, Horse–Buffalo, Cow–Tiger, Rat–Cat, Serpent–Mongoose, Dog–Deer.
- Same yoni is best. A different but non-hostile yoni is neutral. Hostile yonis are avoided.

**7.9 Rasi (Moon-sign relation)** (KP XIII pp.73–74)
- Groom's sign 2nd from the bride's (bride's 12th from groom's): fatal. The converse (bride's sign 2nd from groom's) prolongs life.
- Groom 3rd from bride: misery. Converse: happy life.
- Groom 4th from bride: poverty. Converse: wealth.
- Groom 5th from bride: widowhood. Converse: immunity from widowhood.
- Groom 6th from bride: loss of sons. Converse: birth of sons.
- 7th/7th (mutually opposite): long and happy life.
- Exception: groom 2nd from bride is good if that sign is even, and harmful if odd.
- Exception: groom 6th from bride is good for the pairs Aries–Virgo, Sagittarius–Taurus, Libra–Pisces, Aquarius–Cancer, Leo–Capricorn and Gemini–Scorpio.

**7.10 Rasyadhipathi (sign-lord friendship)** (KP XIII pp.74–75)
- Mars: friends Mercury and Venus; the others are enemies.
- Venus: all are friends except the Sun and Moon.
- Mercury: all are friends except the Sun.
- Moon: friends Mercury and Jupiter.
- Sun: Jupiter is his only friend.
- Jupiter: all are friends except Mars.
- Saturn: Jupiter is his enemy.
- This friendship list is specific to the porutham tradition. It differs from the BPHS/Saravali natural friendships. Do not merge the two.

**7.11 Vasyam** (KP XIII p.75)
- Concordant signs:
  - Leo and Scorpio → Aries.
  - Cancer and Leo → Taurus.
  - Virgo → Gemini.
  - Scorpio and Sagittarius → Cancer.
  - Libra → Leo.
  - Gemini and Pisces → Virgo.
  - Capricorn → Libra.
  - Virgo and Cancer → Scorpio.
  - Pisces → Sagittarius.
  - Aquarius and Aries → Capricorn.
  - Aries → Aquarius.
  - Capricorn → Pisces.
- The groom's Moon sign should be concordant to the bride's, or vice versa, for harmony.

**7.12 Rajju** (KP XIII p.75)
- Five groups:
  - Padha: Aswini, Aslesha, Magha, Jyeshta, Mula, Revathi.
  - Ooroo: Bharani, Pushya, Purvapalguni, Anuradha, Purvashada, Utharabhadrapada.
  - Nabhi: Krithika, Punarvasu, Utharapalguni, Visakha, Utharashada, Purvabhadrapada.
  - Kanta: Rohini, Ardhra, Hastha, Swathi, Sravana, Sathabis.
  - Siro: Mrigasirsha, Chithra, Sravishta.
- Both stars in the same group is forbidden. Results:
  - Padha: travel in foreign lands.
  - Ooroo: loss of property.
  - Nabhi: loss of offspring.
  - Kanta: death of the woman.
  - Siro: death of the man.

**7.13 Vedhai** (KP XIII p.76)
- Mutually repellent pairs, where no marriage should be made: Aswini–Jyeshta, Bharani–Anuradha, Krittika–Visakha, Rohini–Swathi, Ardhra–Sravana, Punarvasu–Utharashada, Pushya–Purvashada, Aslesha–Mula, Magha–Revathi, Purvapalguni–Utharabhadrapada, Utharapalguni–Purvabhadrapada, Hastha–Sathabis, Mrigasirsha–Chithra–Sravishta.
- Exception: Rajju, Vedhai, Ganam and Rasi faults may be ignored if any one of these holds:
  1. The lords of the two Moon signs are the same.
  2. The lords are friends.
  3. The Moon signs are mutually opposite.

**7.14 Weighting and minimum** (KP XIII p.76)
- Dhinam, Ganam, Yoni, Rasi and Rajju are the five chief points, and Dhinam and Rajju come first.
- Benefits of each point:
  - Dhinam: good luck.
  - Ganam: wealth.
  - Mahendhram: attachment.
  - Sthree-Dheergham: welfare.
  - Yoni: sons.
  - Rasi: family growth.
  - Rasyadhipathi: prosperity in sons.
  - Vasyam: posterity.
  - Rajju: felicity and immunity from widowhood.
  - Vedhai: many sons.
- The couple must agree in at least five of the ten. Otherwise decide by omen (sakhunam).
- SEEN note: this is a count threshold in the text. Record it as the text's rule, and do not turn it into a SEEN score (t581).

## #18 Chandra yogas

**18.1 Sunapha / Anapha / Durudhara** (BPHS ch.37 vv.7–10, p.385)
- Any planet other than the Sun in the 2nd from the Moon forms Sunapha. In the 12th it forms Anapha. Planets in both form Durudhara.
- Results: Sunapha gives self-earned wealth, intelligence and fame. Anapha gives health, virtue and fame. Durudhara gives pleasures, wealth and conveyances.

**18.2 Kemadruma** (BPHS ch.37 vv.11–13, p.386)
- Formed when, excluding the Sun, no planet is with the Moon or in the 2nd or 12th from it, and none is in an angle from the Moon or from the ascendant.
- Result: reproach, poverty, perils.

**18.3 Kemadruma, Saravali version** (SAR ch.13 v.2, p.38)
- Formed if none of Sunapha, Anapha or Durudhura is present, or the Moon's angles are empty, or the Moon is unaspected by all planets.
- This conflicts with BPHS 37.11–13, which also requires the kendras from lagna to be empty and does not use aspect. Keep both definitions separate.

**18.4 Saravali Sunapha/Anapha/Durudhura definitions and refinements** (SAR ch.13 vv.1, 3, 8–9, p.38)
- v.1 gives the same definitions as BPHS.
- v.3: there are 30 Sunapha, 30 Anapha and 180 Durudhura permutations.
- vv.8–9: the results depend on the Moon's state (full, new and so on), the strength of the forming planet, and the native's country and race.
- Results by each planet: vv.10–25, pp.38–39.

**18.5 Adhi yoga from the Moon** (BPHS ch.37 v.5, p.385)
- Benefics in the 6th, 7th and 8th from the Moon.
- Result: king, minister or army chief, according to the planets' strength.

**18.6 Dhana yoga from the Moon** (BPHS ch.37 v.6, p.385)
- All three benefics in upachayas from the Moon (3rd, 6th, 10th, 11th) make one very affluent. Two give medium wealth, and one gives negligible wealth.

**18.7 Gaja Kesari** (BPHS ch.36 vv.3–4, p.368)
- Jupiter in an angle from the ascendant or from the Moon, and conjunct or aspected by a benefic, while avoiding debilitation, combustion and an inimical sign.
- Result: splendour, wealth, intelligence, royal favour.
- Santhanam's note on the same page: Jupiter in a kendra from the Moon alone is the common version, and Jataka Parijata gives two kinds. These are translator notes, not verse.

## #19 Surya yogas (found on the same page)
**19.1 Vesi / Vosi / Ubhayachari** (BPHS ch.38 v.1, p.386)
- Excluding the Moon, a planet in the 2nd from the Sun forms Vesi. In the 12th it forms Vosi. Planets in both form Ubhayachari.
- The effects follow in vv.2–3, which were not fully verified.

## #12 Combustion, war, retrogression

**12.1 Combustion orbs** (BPHS Santhanam's notes, ch.7 region, p.100; chapter inferred because ch.8 begins on p.105)
- Direct motion: Moon 12°, Mars 17°, Mercury 14°, Jupiter 11°, Venus 10°, Saturn 16°.
- Retrograde motion: Mercury 12°, Jupiter 11°, Venus 8°, Saturn 16°. The page also prints 8° for Mars, so check it on the image.
- Rahu and Ketu are never combust.
- Santhanam says Venus and Saturn do lose strength when combust.
- This is the translator's note, not Parasara's verse. Cite it as "Santhanam, note".

**12.2 Combustion and war in strength** (SAR ch.4 v.36, p.14)
- Motional strength goes to a planet that wins a planetary war, is retrograde, or is not combust.
- Day-strong planets: Sun, Jupiter, Venus. Night-strong planets: Saturn, Moon, Mars. Mercury is strong always.
- Malefics are strong in the dark half and benefics in the bright half.

**12.3 Combust or defeated planets turn malefic** (SAR ch.5 v.27, p.18)
- A planet that is combust, dim, debilitated, in an enemy's camp, or defeated in war loses its power to do good and gives malefic results in full.

**12.4 Retrograde effects** (SAR ch.5 v.39, p.18)
- A retrograde benefic is strong and can confer kingdom. A retrograde malefic causes grief and purposeless wandering.
- Also SAR ch.5 v.14: some hold that a planet retrograde while in exaltation produces no good.

**12.5 Avasthas tied to combustion and war** (SAR ch.5 vv.2–4, p.15)
- Combust: Vikala. Defeated in war: Nipidita.

## #13 Drishti

**13.1 Graha drishti** (BPHS ch.26 vv.2–5, pp.254–255)
- Aspects on the 3rd/10th, 5th/9th, 4th/8th and 7th increase in quarter steps: 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, full. Effects are proportional.
- All planets aspect the 7th fully.
- Special full aspects: Saturn on the 3rd and 10th, Jupiter on the 5th and 9th, Mars on the 4th and 8th.
- vv.6–8 (p.255) give the exact Drishti-value arithmetic from the longitude difference. Read it on the page before implementing.

**13.2 Saravali aspects** (SAR ch.4 vv.32–33, p.14)
- Gives the same special aspects and the full 7th.
- The source document lost the fraction glyphs (it prints "?" for the 3rd/10th and 5th/9th values). Use BPHS 26.2–5 for those values.

**13.3 Rasi drishti** (BPHS ch.8 vv.1–3, p.105)
- Movable signs aspect the three fixed signs except the adjacent one. Fixed signs aspect the three movable signs except the adjacent one. Dual signs aspect the other three dual signs.
- A planet aspects whatever its sign aspects.

## #5 Saturn transit from the natal Moon (source for the "sade sati" model): PD ch.26 vv.22–23, Ojha pp.635–637
- Counted from the natal Moon sign (janma rashi):
  - 1st: disease, impurity from a death (ashaucha).
  - 2nd: loss of wealth and children.
  - 3rd: gain of position, servants and wealth (good).
  - 4th: loss through wife and relatives, loss of wealth.
  - 5th: loss of wealth, trouble to children, loss of mental peace.
  - 6th: all happiness, victory over enemies (good).
  - 7th: wife's illness or quarrels, fear, futile and painful travel.
  - 8th: fully bad; loss of children, cattle, friends and wealth, health worry.
  - 9th: poverty, obstacles to dharma, death of a father-like elder, constant sorrow.
  - 10th: loss of honour, disease, failing ventures, disrepute.
  - 11th: many kinds of wealth, high fame (good).
  - 12th: wasted effort on futile work, loss by enemies, illness to wife and sons.
- PD 26.22–23 gives Saturn's result by house only.
- **The names "Sade Sati", "Ashtama Shani", "Ardhashtama Shani" and "Kantaka Shani" are post-classical. They do not appear in this text.** The SEEN model may group 12th+1st+2nd as "Sade Sati", 8th as Ashtama and 4th as Ardhashtama, but it must cite PD 26.22–23 for the results and label the names as post-classical.
- Related on p.637: PD 26.25 says the Sun and Mars act in the first third of a sign, Jupiter and Venus in the middle third, and the Moon and Saturn in the last third. Mercury and Rahu act throughout the sign. This matters for Saturn timing.

## #20 Pancha Mahapurusha: BPHS2 ch.75
**20.1 Definition** (vv.1–2, p.924)
- Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus or Saturn in its own sign or exaltation sign, and in a kendra from the ascendant, forms Ruchaka, Bhadra, Hamsa, Malavya or Sasa respectively.
- The kendra is from the ascendant only. The verse does not count kendras from the Moon.

**20.2 Effects** (vv.3–22, pp.925–927)
- Each yoga gives a physical type, character and lifespan:
  - Ruchaka: about 70 years.
  - Bhadra: 100 years.
  - Hamsa: 100 years.
  - Malavya: 70 years.
  - Sasa: 70 years.
- The regions of rule named in the verses (Sahya/Vindhya, Madhya Desa, Ganga–Yamuna, Sindhu/Malwa) are, per Santhanam's note, not identifiable today. Do not render them as places.

## #25 Yogini dasha: BPHS2 ch.46 vv.195–199, p.572
- There are eight yoginis: Mangala, Pingala, Dhanya, Bhramari, Bhadrika, Ulka, Siddha, Sankata.
- Their lords are, in order: Moon, Sun, Jupiter, Mars, Mercury, Saturn, Venus, Rahu.
- Periods are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 years (36 in total).
- Starting yogini: (Janma nakshatra number + 3) mod 8. A remainder of 1 means Mangala, and so on; remainder 0 means Sankata. Worked example on the page: Mrigasira (5) + 3 = 8, remainder 0, so Sankata.
- The balance at birth is found from the elapsed and total portions of the birth star, as in Vimshottari.

## #26 Ashtottari dasha: BPHS2 ch.46
**26.1 Sequence** (vv.17–20, pp.513–514)
- Recommended when Rahu is in a kendra or trikona from the lagna lord but not in the lagna.
- Counting starts from Ardra:
  - The Sun gets 4 stars.
  - Then the Moon 3, Mars 4, Mercury 3, Saturn 4, Jupiter 3, Rahu 4, Venus 3.
- The birth dasha is found by counting to the Janma nakshatra.
- Periods: Sun 6, Moon 15, Mars 8, Mercury 17, Saturn 10, Jupiter 19, Rahu 12, Venus 21 (108 years). Ketu has no dasha.

**26.2 Balance** (vv.21–22, p.514)
- Per the translation, one nakshatra spans 1/4 of the dasha for malefic lords and 1/3 for benefic lords.
- Balance = the expired fraction of the birth star × that star-share, as in Vimshottari.
- Santhanam's special note on p.514: Abhijit = the 4th pada of Uttarashada plus 1/15 of Sravana. Abhijit is used only in Ashtottari and Shashtihayani.

**26.3 Applicability** (v.23, p.516)
- Advisable if birth is by day in Krishna paksha or by night in Shukla paksha.
- This is a second condition, alongside the Rahu condition in vv.17–20. The text gives both; implement them as separate flags.

## #22 Viparita Raja yoga: UK, section "Effects of the Planets and Houses", v.22, p.91
- Three yogas:
  1. The 8th lord in the 6th or 12th.
  2. The 6th lord in the 8th or 12th.
  3. The 12th lord in the 6th or 8th.
- The lords must be related to each other by conjunction, mutual aspect or exchange. At the same time they must have no such relation with other planets.
- Result: famous, prosperous, a powerful king or emperor.
- Translator's note: the relation is only between the dusthana lords. All three involved is most powerful.

## #31 Jaimini Atmakaraka: JS Su. 11, p.13
- Among the seven planets Sun–Saturn, or the eight including Rahu, the one with the most degrees (within its sign) is the Atmakaraka.
- Translator's notes (pp.13–14): Rahu is counted (in reverse, since it moves backward) when two planets tie. Ketu is excluded.
- 7- vs 8-karaka scheme: the sutra gives both. Record it as an option and do not pick one silently.

## #18 (adds) Kemadruma and lunar yogas: JP Adh. VII
**18.8 Kemadruma, JP version** (sl.71, p.422)
- Formed when:
  - the Moon in the 1st or 7th lacks Jupiter's aspect; or
  - the ashtakavarga bindus in the signs occupied by planets are very deficient and all planets are weak.
- This is a third definition, beside BPHS 37.11–13 and SAR 13.2. Keep all three separate.

**18.9 Sunapha / Anapha / Durudhara** (sl.72–73, p.423)
- Same as BPHS: planets other than the Sun in the 2nd, the 12th, or both from the Moon. With none of the three present, give Kemadruma results.
- sl.74 (p.423), Daridra yoga: the Moon conjunct the Sun in a malefic navamsa and aspected by a debilitated planet. Also, at night birth, the 8th from the waning Moon aspected or occupied by a malefic.

## #12 (adds) Combustion orbs, classical: SS ch. IX vv.6–9, p.257
- Degrees of setting (time-degrees in oblique ascension, not ecliptic longitude): Jupiter 11, Saturn 15, Mars 17.
- Venus: 8° for setting in the west and rising in the east (retrograde phase), and 10° for setting in the east and rising in the west.
- Mercury: 12° when retrograde and 14° when advancing fast.
- The Moon is treated in the next chapter.
- Closer than these, a planet is invisible ("swallowed").
- **This conflicts with Santhanam's note table (BPHS p.100, #12.1), which gives Saturn 16°.** The classical source is SS (Saturn 15°). Also, SS measures time-degrees, not longitude. Keep the source of each orb on the field.

## #43 Seventh house / couple rules: JP Adh. XIV
**43.1** (sl.4, p.846)
- Lords of the 10th, 2nd and 7th in the 10th: affairs outside marriage.
- Lords of the 5th, 9th and 7th in dusthanas, with the 6th aspected by a malefic: childless.
- Jupiter+Mercury or Moon+Venus in the 7th: addicted to many women.
- A strong Venus in the 7th: many wives.

**43.2** (sl.5, p.846)
- Venus and Mars in the 7th: bereft of a wife. The same holds if they are in the 9th and 6th.
- Moon and Venus in the 6th: only one son.
- Malefics in the 1st, 7th and 12th, with a weak Moon in the 5th: a barren wife, or wifeless.
- Notes on p.849 (cited from other texts, not JP verse):
  - The 7th lord in the 8th, or the 5th or 8th lord in the 7th: loss of wife.
  - Sun and Rahu in the 7th: loss of wealth through women.

**43.3 Female chart** (JP, female-horoscopy adhyaya, sl.61–62, p.963)
- Read the husband from the 7th of the female chart.
- The Moon in the 7th: a handsome, voluptuous husband troubled by ailments.
- Mars in the 7th: a husband humble in appearance, fond of cruelty, indolent, quick-tongued and ruddy.
- sl.61 links the husband's longevity and status to the 7th and 8th lords' placement: trikona or kendra gives long life and wealth, and the various associations give the different traits.
- Caveat: this is Layer-C text about personality, so gate it carefully in user-facing copy.

## #23 Nabhasa yogas: BJ ch. XII
**23.1 Counts** (v.1, p.126)
- Four groups, 32 yogas in all: Akriti 20, Sankhya 7, Asraya 3, Dala 2.
- Per Varahamihira, the Yavanas give 1,800 Nabhasa yogas.

## #12 (adds) Planetary war: SS ch. VII "Of Planetary Conjunctions", vv.18–23, pp.199–200

## #40 (completes) Kakshya: verse text, PD ch.23 vv.15–19, Ojha p.553 (header "chapter 23: Ashtakavarga")
- **v.16:** to determine the time when a bindu's result comes, divide each sign into eight kakshyas. "Others" give the results by kakshya in order. Mantreswara attributes this to others.
- **v.17:** draw the prastara chart with 9 horizontal and 13 (10 + 3) vertical lines, giving 96 cells: 8 rows × 12 signs.
- **v.18:** the rows from the south are Lagna, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn. Each gives its bindu result in order.
- **v.19:** Saturn gives the result in the first eighth of a sign, Jupiter in the second, and so on in kakshya order, with Lagna in the last. That makes the order from 0°: Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Lagna, which matches Ojha p.556 (batch 9b).
- **Ojha on v.15 (p.553):** when a planet transits a house and that sign holds more bindus in the planet's own ashtakavarga, that house's matters bring special good. Example: the Sun, Jupiter or Saturn transiting the 10th from their natal sign, with many bindus there.
