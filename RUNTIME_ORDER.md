# RUNTIME ORDER

One sequence. Do not hunt other files for what to do next. Other files are reference. This file is the order.

Do not show a later step on the phone until this step is finished.

## 1. Name

Who is being read. Self, parent, child, ex, friend. Store the name. Stop.

## 2. Day

Birth date. Store the date. Do not calculate yet.

## 3. Birth city

Search. The pick must carry latitude and longitude. Store the city. Do not calculate yet.

## 4. Places lived

Six months or more. Shorter stays do not count. Each place has a start year and an end year. Store them. Do not use them to change the sky.

## 5. Day is given — Western only

Input is name, date, birth city lat/long. Clock is unknown until a later step.

Run Swiss Ephemeris for that day and that city. Positions only. No houses if the clock is unknown. Do not guess noon.

Do not run BaZi, galactic, Jyotish, Hellenistic, Tzolk’in, or numerology in this step. They are not ready.

## 6. Place is given — that place only

Input is one stored place plus its years.

Say what that place was for this person in those years. Do not blend two places into one person. Do not turn the place into one number.

## 7. Clock

Only after 5. Three passes if the clock is unknown: 04:00, 12:00, 20:00. Each pass is its own run. Do not pick a winner in this step.

## 8. Other person

Same steps 1–4 for that person. Their run stays theirs. Do not mix the two skies.

## 9. Say it

Only after a run exists. Say what that run found. Their story about the other person first, if that is why they came. Then the same sight on them.

No mask. No grade. No later chapter on this screen.
