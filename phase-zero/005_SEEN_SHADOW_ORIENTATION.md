# 005_SEEN_SHADOW_ORIENTATION

This file defines the app orientation around shadow recognition.

SEEN begins from the principle that the shadow a person refuses to see does not disappear. It becomes an unseen organizer of behavior, attraction, conflict, projection, repetition, and inherited relational harm.

## Core Orientation

The shadow that is ignored becomes the force that quietly governs the life.

Traits denied in the self do not vanish.

They return as:

- projection onto other people
- attraction to familiar relational wounds
- repeated conflict loops
- moral certainty without self-contact
- inherited family patterns
- unexamined parenting climate
- hidden resentment
- addiction to familiar emotional states
- avoidance disguised as personality
- control disguised as care
- collapse disguised as compassion

What is refused consciously is often repeated unconsciously.

## App Entry Purpose

The app does not begin by flattering the user.

The app begins by helping the user recognize the pattern that has been running beneath the surface.

This is why shadow orientation belongs before advice, affirmation, manifestation, sovereignty, or tracking.

Recognition comes first.

Sovereignty comes after recognition.

Tracking comes after the user has something real to track.

## Relational Orientation

Users may enter through another person:

- an ex-partner
- a parent
- a caregiver
- a partner
- a family member
- a person who harmed, abandoned, confused, or shaped them

The goal is not blame.

The goal is to see how a person’s patterns may have been formed before the relationship began.

This creates context without excusing harm.

It separates the user’s worth from another person’s unresolved pattern.

## Shadow Rule

What is not owned will be acted out, projected, pursued, defended, or passed forward.

SEEN names the shadow so the user can stop serving it unconsciously.

## User-Facing Translation Rule

The app should not expose raw mechanics first.

Do not begin with labels, charts, houses, markers, gates, or technical language.

Begin with recognizable human pattern language:

- what keeps repeating
- what gets denied
- what gets projected
- what gets chased
- what gets avoided
- what it costs
- how it affects others
- how it becomes inherited
- where agency can return

## Orientation Sequence

The orientation layer follows this sequence:

1. Name the repeated pattern.
2. Show the hidden shadow beneath it.
3. Separate blame from recognition.
4. Name the relational cost.
5. Name the inherited or contextual pressure.
6. Return the user to agency.
7. Route into sovereignty, regulation, or tracking.

## Final Lock

The shadow orientation is not punishment.

It is not fatalism.

It is not shame.

It is the first honest mirror.

The app must help the user see what has been running them without turning that recognition into condemnation.
