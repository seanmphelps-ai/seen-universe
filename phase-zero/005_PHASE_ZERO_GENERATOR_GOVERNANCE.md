# 005_PHASE_ZERO_GENERATOR_GOVERNANCE

This file governs deterministic extraction, convergence, conflict handling, recursive passes, routing, and reproducibility before Oracle narration.

The Generator produces finalized structured payload.

The Oracle does not alter Generator truth.

---

## GENERATOR AUTONOMY LAW

The Generator runs extraction autonomously.

Per portal and wound marker:

1. extract
2. deepen
3. compare delta
4. continue until signal exhaustion
5. route to the next active signal

The Generator determines when a thread is exhausted, when convergence weakens, when routing shifts, and when another portal becomes dominant.

The user does not manually trigger every extraction step.

---

## PRESSURE FIELD LAW

Signals are dynamic.

No wound marker, portal, pattern, environment marker, attachment state, relationship marker, or timing marker exists in isolation.

The Generator must account for:
- amplification
- suppression
- sensitization
- delay
- distortion
- reroute
- recurrence
- split
- environmental modulation

Amplification in one area may suppress, distort, or reroute another.

---

## ENVIRONMENTAL SHAPING LAW

Environmental shaping precedes birth-date interpretation.

The Generator distinguishes:
- intrinsic baseline
- environmental adaptation
- survival conditioning
- nervous-system shaping
- relational imprinting
- present-state modulation

Behavior is interpreted through pressure context.

Biome, abiotic, and GeoPresence remain independent source fields.

They may converge.

They may not be collapsed into one another.

---

## CONFLICT RESOLUTION LAW

When signals conflict, priority follows this order:

1. regulation instability
2. environmental mismatch
3. highest relational or body cost
4. repeated convergence
5. timing activation layers

Not all signals carry equal weight.

Contradiction remains visible internally until resolved or intentionally preserved as unresolved.

The Generator must not flatten contradiction into false certainty.

---

## SIGNAL EXHAUSTION LAW

Recursive extraction continues until:
- no meaningful new convergence appears
- no new pressure visibility appears
- no new behavioral consequence appears
- no new routing relevance appears

The Generator determines exhaustion internally.

The Oracle receives only finalized extraction output.

---

## RECURSIVE PASS LAW

Every recursive pass must preserve prior structure, prior signal, and convergence integrity.

Each pass may only add:
- clarity
- pressure visibility
- relational mapping
- body impact
- behavioral consequence
- regulation insight
- deeper convergence
- stronger routing precision

Recursive passes must not restart interpretation, flatten contradictions, replace prior payload, or invent unsupported certainty.

---

## DIRECTION ROUTING LAW

The Generator routes toward the next highest-value unresolved signal.

Routing priority is determined by:
- active cost
- convergence density
- regulation relevance
- relational impact
- body impact
- recurrence relevance
- Life Section activation
- portal activation strength

Routing is deterministic from finalized source fields and recorded weighting rules.

---

## MODEL-SWAP REPRODUCIBILITY LAW

Render-model changes may alter language only.

They may not alter:
- source payload
- normalized scores
- convergence state
- conflict-resolution outcome
- portal activation
- Life Section deposits
- traversal route
- recursive extraction result

A different narration model must receive the same finalized Generator payload for the same validated inputs and version set.

---

## FAILSAFE LAW

When confidence is weak, the Generator must:
- preserve unresolved status
- reduce claim eligibility
- lower routing priority where appropriate
- retain contradictory evidence
- prevent deterministic surface claims
- mark incomplete convergence

Low-confidence signal may contribute pressure.

It may not masquerade as settled truth.

---

## VERSIONING LAW

Every major generation records:
- schema version
- engine version
- scoring version
- routing version
- Oracle version
- render version

Stored outputs must remain traceable and reproducible across revisions.

---

## OUTPUT FINALIZATION LAW

Before Oracle handoff, the Generator must finalize:
- validated source fields
- normalized markers
- confidence state
- contradictions
- convergence groups
- portal deposits
- Life Section deposits
- wound-pressure deposits
- routing state
- traversal candidates
- unresolved variables
- version metadata

The Oracle receives finalized payload only.

The Oracle does not perform raw source inference.

---

## GENERATOR / ORACLE BOUNDARY

The Generator determines:
- what exists
- what converges
- what conflicts
- what activates
- what repeats
- what routes
- what remains unresolved

The Oracle determines:
- how to pace
- how to stabilize
- when to reveal
- how to translate
- when to challenge
- when to regulate

The Generator may not perform emotional theater.

The Oracle may not alter deterministic outcomes.

---

## FINAL LOCK

Generator governance controls convergence, recursive extraction, conflict resolution, signal exhaustion, weighting, scoring outcomes, routing, deterministic payloads, and reproducibility.

Oracle governance controls narration, pacing, containment, traversal delivery, and regulated human translation.

The two layers remain distinct and connected by validated handoff.
