# 004_SEEN_CADENCE_CONTINUITY_AND_SHARING_GUARDRAILS

Cadence begins after recognition.

Cadence is the continuous operational layer of SEEN.

It translates what SEEN discovers into daily guidance, tracking, regulation, and relational continuity.

## Cadence Continuity

Cadence may include:

- daily SEEN Protocol forecast
- emotional tracking
- behavioral tracking
- coherence checks
- gratitude practice
- breathwork
- somatic release
- relational prompts
- regulation reminders
- communication repair prompts
- sovereignty reinforcement

## Daily SEEN Protocol Forecast

The daily SEEN Protocol maps:

- current astrology
- user baseline
- known wound patterns
- attachment patterns
- emotional tendencies
- behavioral loops
- environmental pressures
- relationship context
- tracked history

into practical daily guidance.

It shows what the user may feel, repeat, avoid, amplify, suppress, or need to regulate.

## Shareable Pattern Rule

SEEN supports shareable patterns from the beginning.

Sharing is contextual.

Sharing is permission-based.

Sharing is role-based.

The system may share differently with:

- spouse
- partner
- family member
- close friend
- potential mate
- support person

## Sharing Guardrails

The user controls:

- what is shared
- who sees it
- how much they see
- how long they see it
- whether daily check-ins are visible
- whether journal content is visible
- whether emotional state summaries are visible
- whether deeper mechanics are visible

## Relational Visibility Purpose

The purpose of sharing is relational clarity.

A partner may see enough context to understand:

- rough day
- emotional pressure
- regulation need
- communication capacity
- relational sensitivity
- withdrawal risk
- repair need

The goal is reduced confusion, reduced masking, clearer communication, and better relational timing.

## No-Mask Principle

SEEN values congruence.

The goal is reduced discrepancy between presented self and lived behavior.

The system supports honesty, clarity, communication, and choice.

Sharing must remain consent-based.
