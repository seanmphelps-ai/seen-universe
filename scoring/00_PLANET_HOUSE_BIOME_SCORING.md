# PLANET HOUSE BIOME SCORING

A single system handles all planets and all houses.

PLANET = TYPE OF PRESSURE
HOUSE = WHERE IT EXPRESSES
BIOME = FORCE MULTIPLIER / DISTORTER

Everything is scored the same way, then weighted differently.

CORE SCORING GRID:

- Intrinsic Pressure Score (IPS)
- House Expression Score (HES)
- Biome Modulation Score (BMS)
- Delta between intrinsic vs lived reality

Final Output = IPS × HES × BMS

HOUSE PRIORITY:

- 4th house
- 8th house
- 10th house
- 11th house
- 12th house

BIOME COMPATIBILITY INDEX:

1. Survival Match
2. Energy Cost
3. Threat Exposure
4. Recovery Access

INTERPRETATION RULE:

- High intrinsic + low biome compatibility = latent strength / real-world struggle
- Low intrinsic + high biome compatibility = average system / stable life
- High + high = peak alignment
- Low + low = chronic instability
