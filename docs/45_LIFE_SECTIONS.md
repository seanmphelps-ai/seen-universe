# 45 LIFE SECTIONS REGISTRY

Version: 1.0.0
Status: CANONICAL — do not invent beyond this list.

Every portal deposit, wound result, lens result, environmental signal, attachment finding, childhood triangle result, drama triangle result, Jungian inversion, recognition summary, and Oracle reveal carries one or more valid routes from this registry.

A section is a **destination**, not a calculation. The Generator deposits evidence into sections. The Oracle renders from sections. No section is skipped because it is empty — empty sections are visible as silence, which is itself information.

---

## 01 — ORIGIN & LINEAGE
Where the person came from. Family structure, ancestral patterns, migration, the room they were born into, the people who shaped the first years. The soil before the seed had a name for itself.

## 02 — BIRTH & EARLY IMPRINT
The moment of arrival and the first environment. Hospital or home, season, light, the first sounds, the first hands. What the body learned before language.

## 03 — CHILDHOOD SAFETY
Whether the child felt protected. What produced safety — a parent, a routine, a hiding place, silence, compliance. What threatened it. The adaptation that formed around the answer.

## 04 — CHILDHOOD BELONGING
Whether the child felt included. What earned a place at the table — being good, being useful, being invisible, being the funny one, being the strong one. What exclusion felt like and what it taught.

## 05 — CHILDHOOD REWARD
What got noticed, praised, or loved. Achievement, obedience, beauty, suffering, silence, performance. The reward system that became the adult's operating logic.

## 06 — ATTACHMENT STYLE
How the person bonds, detaches, pursues, withdraws, tests, clings, or controls in close relationships. Derived from Moon, Venus, Saturn, 4th/7th/8th house, and lived experience — never diagnosed from planets alone.

## 07 — LOVE LANGUAGE & RELATIONAL EXPRESSION
How love is given, received, withheld, performed, or weaponized. What the person does when they love someone and what they do when they are afraid of losing them.

## 08 — INTIMACY & SEXUALITY
How closeness is approached, avoided, or controlled. What intimacy threatens. Where desire and fear collide. The body as the site of both connection and danger.

## 09 — PARTNERSHIP DYNAMICS
How the person behaves inside a committed bond. Power, equality, conflict, repair, abandonment, control. What they do to a partner and what a partner does to them.

## 10 — CONFLICT & ANGER
How anger surfaces — explosive, silent, delayed, redirected, suppressed until it leaks. What triggers it. What it costs. What it protects.

## 11 — FEAR & ANXIETY
The dominant fear structures. Abandonment, engulfment, failure, exposure, loss of control, annihilation. How fear organizes daily behavior.

## 12 — TRUST & BETRAYAL
Whether trust is given, withheld, tested, or broken. The betrayal history — real or perceived — that shapes every subsequent relationship. The cost of trusting and the cost of not trusting.

## 13 — SELF-WORTH & IDENTITY
How the person values themselves. Whether worth is earned, performed, or assumed. The gap between self-perception and how others experience them.

## 14 — SELF-SABOTAGE & DESTRUCTIVE LOOPS
The patterns that build something and then destroy it. The trigger, the mechanism, the reset, the repeat. What gets burned and why.

## 15 — CONTROL & POWER
How the person seeks, holds, loses, or surrenders control. Power over self, over others, over outcomes. Where control becomes protection and where it becomes prison.

## 16 — AUTHORITY & HIERARCHY
How the person relates to authority — defers, rebels, mimics, resents, or becomes. The authority figures who shaped them and the authority they now exercise or refuse.

## 17 — WORK & CAREER
How the person functions in work. Ambition, avoidance, overwork, underperformance, perfectionism, burnout. What work means — survival, identity, escape, proof.

## 18 — MONEY & RESOURCES
The relationship to money. Scarcity, abundance, hoarding, spending, avoidance, shame. What money represents — safety, freedom, worth, control.

## 19 — HOME & DOMESTIC LIFE
How the person creates or avoids a home. The domestic environment as sanctuary or battlefield. What "home" means and whether it exists.

## 20 — FAMILY OF ORIGIN WOUNDS
The specific injuries from the family system. Parent patterns, sibling dynamics, generational trauma, the roles assigned in childhood that persist into adulthood.

## 21 — PARENTING & CARETAKING
How the person parents or cares for others. Whether they repeat, repair, or refuse the patterns they inherited. The cost of caretaking and the cost of not caretaking.

## 22 — FRIENDSHIP & COMMUNITY
How the person connects outside romantic bonds. Loyalty, isolation, social performance, genuine belonging. What friendship costs and what it gives.

## 23 — SOCIAL MASK & PERSONA
The face presented to the world. What is hidden behind it. The gap between the mask and the person underneath. When the mask slips and what happens.

## 24 — EMOTIONAL REGULATION
How emotions are processed, suppressed, flooded, or expressed. The nervous system baseline — calm, reactive, hypervigilant, numb. What regulates and what dysregulates.

## 25 — GRIEF & LOSS
How loss is experienced and processed. Delayed, ritualized, denied, or overwhelming. What has been lost and what has not been grieved. The unfinished losses that still run the system.

## 26 — BODY & SOMATIC EXPRESSION
How the body carries what the mind cannot. Tension, illness, numbness, hypervigilance, collapse. The body as the archive of unprocessed experience.

## 27 — HEALTH & VITALITY
Physical health patterns. Chronic conditions, stress-related illness, neglect of the body, over-care of the body. What the body is trying to say.

## 28 — MENTAL HEALTH & PSYCHOLOGICAL PATTERNS
Depression, anxiety, dissociation, hypervigilance, perfectionism, rumination. The psychological architecture and its costs. Not diagnosis — pattern recognition.

## 29 — ADDICTION & COMPULSIVE BEHAVIOR
Substances, behaviors, people, work, control, avoidance. What is being numbed, escaped, or controlled. The cycle and the cost.

## 30 — CREATIVITY & EXPRESSION
How the person creates, performs, or expresses. Whether creativity is free or controlled, shared or hidden, celebrated or punished. What blocks it and what releases it.

## 31 — SPIRITUALITY & MEANING
The search for meaning, purpose, connection to something larger. Whether spirituality is genuine, performative, avoided, or weaponized. The existential questions that drive behavior.

## 32 — BELIEF SYSTEMS & WORLDVIEW
The beliefs that organize reality. Inherited, chosen, rejected, or clung to. How beliefs protect and how they imprison. What happens when a belief is challenged.

## 33 — DECISION-MAKING & AGENCY
How decisions are made — impulsively, avoidantly, over-analyzed, delegated, controlled. Whether the person feels they have agency or feels acted upon. The cost of each mode.

## 34 — BOUNDARIES & LIMITS
Whether boundaries exist, are respected, are violated, or are weaponized. The line between self and other. Where it is held and where it collapses.

## 35 — VULNERABILITY & OPENNESS
Whether the person can be seen. What vulnerability costs and what it gives. The armor and what is underneath it. When the armor comes off and what happens.

## 36 — RECEPTIVITY & DEPENDENCE
Whether the person can receive — help, love, care, rest. The difficulty of depending on others. What receiving threatens. The cost of self-sufficiency.

## 37 — GIVING & OVER-GIVING
Whether the person gives too much, too little, or the right amount. What giving protects. The cost of over-giving and the cost of withholding.

## 38 — ISOLATION & WITHDRAWAL
When and why the person withdraws. Whether isolation is chosen, forced, or compulsive. What it protects and what it costs. The difference between solitude and abandonment.

## 39 — PUBLIC LIFE & VISIBILITY
How the person shows up in public. Performance, avoidance, dominance, invisibility. What visibility costs and what it gives. The gap between public and private self.

## 40 — PRIVATE LIFE & INNER WORLD
The inner life — thoughts, fantasies, fears, desires that no one sees. Whether the inner world is rich or barren, safe or dangerous. What lives there that has never been spoken.

## 41 — TIME & TEMPORALITY
How the person relates to time. Past-bound, future-anxious, present-absent. Whether time is an enemy, a resource, or a mystery. The temporal patterns that shape behavior.

## 42 — CHANGE & TRANSITION
How the person handles change. Resistance, collapse, adaptation, growth. What transitions trigger and what they cost. The pattern of entering and leaving.

## 43 — SUCCESS & ACHIEVEMENT
What success means and whether it is pursued, avoided, or sabotaged. The relationship between achievement and worth. What happens after success and what happens after failure.

## 44 — FAILURE & SHAME
How failure is experienced. Whether it is survived, denied, or weaponized. The shame that follows and the beliefs it reinforces. What failure teaches.

## 45 — LEGACY & COMPLETION
What the person leaves behind. Whether they think about legacy, death, or ending. What remains unfinished. What they want to be remembered for and what they fear being remembered for.

## Routing Rules

1. Every deposit carries a `lifeSectionId` (1–45), a `weight` (0–1), and a `reason`.
2. One signal may route to multiple sections.
3. One section may receive from multiple signals.
4. Empty sections are not errors — they are visible silence.
5. The Oracle may render any section, populated or empty, but must not invent content for an empty section.
6. No section is privileged over another. The person's life decides which sections are loud.
