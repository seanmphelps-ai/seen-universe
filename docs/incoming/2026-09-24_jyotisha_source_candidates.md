## Comparison, dependencies and open conflicts

- The earlier phase-one shelf names BPHS, *Phaladīpikā*, *Light on Life*, and Swiss/Lahiri/CRC/IMD without edition-and-passage locks. Keep it as a candidate lead; do not merge it into a school canon. *Light on Life* is a modern interpretation, not the original-language primary witness.
- `JY-C01` describes Varāhamihira. `JY-C02` describes Mantreśvara. `JY-C03` proposes a Parāśarī textual packet. A shared word such as *daśā* does not resolve formulas or make these schools interchangeable. No school chosen here; `JY-C05` might change coverage after inspection.
- Scientific computation depends on `JY-C04` plus a specified input/ephemeris configuration. Named-school rule extraction depends on edition, scan/verse mapping and independently reconstructed complete technique inventory. Native reading depends on both; independent audit follows all handoffs before delivery.
- **Worked example gate remains open:** after source, mode and rule lock, record one actual date/time/place/time zone → UTC → sidereal graha/Moon degrees and ayanāṃśa → nakṣatra/pāda (only under the locked scheme) → applicable daśā/birth balance (only under the locked family) → per-rule testimony → reading. Independently compute the same values and test a Moon cusp/pāda boundary, uncertain birth time, and a second ayanāṃśa. Log each input, intermediate result, conflicting witness and `NOT_RUN` reason. There is no validated worked case in this packet.
- Rights and license checks remain open for source transcriptions, modern translations and public deployment of Swiss Ephemeris. The [official Swiss guide](https://www.astro.com/swisseph/swephprg.htm) states AGPL or Professional license alternatives for its library.
