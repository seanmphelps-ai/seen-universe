## Sources (verified)

### 1. Vettius Valens — *Anthologies* (primary, 2nd c. CE)
- **Citation:** Mark T. Riley, preliminary English translation (1990s), based on Kroll 1908 + Pingree 1986 Greek editions. Skyscript digital hosting.
- **URL checked:** landing https://www.skyscript.co.uk/valens_riley.html (fetched OK); PDF https://www.skyscript.co.uk/pdf/pubs/texts/valens/riley/docs/Vettius_Valens_Riley.pdf (fetched OK — opens with Book I planetary natures).
- **Why valid:** Largest surviving Hellenistic practice manual; Fortune/Spirit, whole-sign place language, chronocrators, worked charts. Riley is the open scholarly English path (not a blog paraphrase).
- **Use:** **CALC** (lot formulas, place counting) + **INTERPRETATION** (wound/outcome language) + **RENDERING** (provenance wording).
- **Contested/weak:** Riley labels preliminary/unperfected; mathematical passages may err; always cross-check Pingree Greek or Schmidt Project Hindsight for contested math.

### 2. Dorotheus of Sidon — *Carmen Astrologicum* (primary, 1st c. CE via Arabic)
- **Citation:** David Pingree, *Dorothei Sidonii Carmen Astrologicum* (Teubner, 1976); English from Arabic + Greek/Latin fragments. Book index: http://www.attalus.org/info/dorotheus.html (fetched OK).
- **Why valid:** Core Hellenistic/early transmission for nativities, length of life, year transfers; Pingree is standard critical edition/translator.
- **Use:** **CALC** (timing / year-transfer scaffolding) + **INTERPRETATION** (topics, parents, marriage strands).
- **Contested/weak:** Survives mainly via Arabic; verse→prose transmission loss; not a clean Greek autograph.

### 3. Chris Brennan — *Hellenistic Astrology: The Study of Fate and Fortune* (Amor Fati, 2017)
- **Citation:** ISBN 9780998588902; Google Books record verified: https://books.google.com/books/about/Hellenistic_Astrology.html?id=GjI8DgAAQBAJ (TOC shows Ch. 10 Twelve Places, Ch. 11 House Division, Ch. 16 Lots, Ch. 18–19 Profections / Zodiacal Releasing).
- **Why valid:** Modern scholarly reconstruction citing primary corpus; standard SEEN-class secondary for whole-sign implementation and Fortune/Spirit articulation.
- **Use:** **CALC** (implementation rules) + **INTERPRETATION** (sect, places, lots) + **RENDERING** (clear English for Portal packets).
- **Contested/weak:** Secondary synthesis — never override Valens/Dorotheus/Paulus when they conflict; house-division chapter itself documents the debate.

### 4. Paulus Alexandrinus — Hermetic Lots (via Project Hindsight / documented formulas)
- **Citation path checked:** Detailed formula documentation with Paulus quotes + Schmidt notes at https://www.astrology-x-files.com/x-files/arabic-parts.html (fetched OK). Underlying Project Hindsight: Robert Schmidt trans., *Paulus Alexandrinus: Introductory Matters* (Golden Hind Press).
- **Why valid:** Primary locus for seven Hermetic lots including **Eros** (Venus–Spirit construction); needed for SEEN Eros lot.
- **Use:** **CALC** (Eros after Spirit) + **INTERPRETATION** (appetite / desire / mutual favor — wounds-first for SEEN).
- **Contested/weak (MUST FLAG):**
  1. **Two Eros traditions:** Paulus/Hermetic (Spirit↔Venus) vs Valens (Fortune↔Spirit arc). Different points. SEEN default = Paulus/Hermetic unless packet says Valens.
  2. **Schmidt correction:** manuscript reverse-by-night vs Schmidt’s “no reverse because Fortune/Spirit already reverse.” Our dark-window run used reverse-by-night Paulus; note both in provenance.
  3. astrology-x-files is a practitioner mirror of Hindsight notes — cite Paulus/Schmidt as authority, not the blog as primary.

### 5. (Calc provenance) Neugebauer & Van Hoesen — *Greek Horoscopes* (APS Memoirs vol. 48, 1959)
- **Citation:** O. Neugebauer & H. B. Van Hoesen; Internet Archive listing https://archive.org/details/greek-horoscopes ; Penn Press catalog https://www.pennpress.org/9780871690487/greek-horoscopes/
- **Why valid:** Critical edition of surviving Greek horoscopes — ground-truth for how positions/places were recorded historically.
- **Use:** **CALC** validation / historical practice check — not SEEN shadow copy.
- **Contested/weak:** Does **not** prove uniform whole-sign-only practice; later charts (e.g. Eutocius L 497) show quadrant cusps (Skyscript/Houlding discussion). Whole-sign remains SEEN default; flag coexistence.
