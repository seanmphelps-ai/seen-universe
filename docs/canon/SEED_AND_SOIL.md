# Seed and soil

Native systems compute first. SEEN does not rewrite them.

1. Run each native system whole. Keep the seed it depicts.
2. Do not blend seeds. Do not correct one system with another.
3. Only after the seed exists does location run — as incubator, not as a new sky.
4. The same seed in two soils is two expressions. The seed does not move.

## Uniqueness

No two charts may share a sentence, summary, or sequence of words.
If a line could sit on another native, delete it and write from this native only.
Examples are never copy. They are shape only.

## Soil is dated

A place is not a place. A place is place + years.

- Castro Valley at 4 is not Castro Valley at 16.
- Los Angeles is not Castro Valley.
- A month that works in one soil can fail in another.
- Two people born a day apart in the same house can meet opposite incubators.
- Two people with the same clock in different birth cities are not the same seed in the same soil.

## Order

Sky / native seed → then soil → then how that seed came out here.
Never soil first. Never a recycled paragraph to stand in for a person.
