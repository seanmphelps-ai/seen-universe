# Portal extraction

Modalities stay whole and untouched in separate cocoons. Each reading is a traditional native translation from a verified, validated source. Do not mix modalities. Do not fold them into shared prose. Do not add new-age interpretation. Do not invent.

Location is the incubator after the intact seed. It shows how that seed will or will not grow in that dirt. It does not rewrite the native reading.

Portal extraction, when present, is a structured field list only:

- triggers
- pressure points
- failure modes
- costs/consequences
- how pressure builds / accumulates
- how it releases
- what typically gets destroyed
- how long they let it go
- show
- defend
- react
- what is lost if it runs one more cycle

These fields are not a license to blend modalities. No two natives share a sentence.

Code shape: `lib/portals/template.ts` (`PORTAL_EXTRACTION_FIELDS`).

## Field

The extraction is not seed alone and not soil alone. The seed stays the seed.

- native seed, untouched
- birth clock and birth city
- lived soils with years
- who is in the room (lover, family, friend, running-with)
- calendar month and clock
- trip type (family, business, alone)
- the other person's seed in that same soil, still whole

JEV is the shape of the field: bodies in a system, each one pulling every other one. The pull does not merge the seeds. Change one body and the extraction fields are filled again from the intact seeds in that dirt.

## Law

All 64 can wake. Activation is the field, not a personality badge.
Native seeds stay whole. The field only decides how they come out here, now, with these people.
