## Canonical language

**Place is the incubator. Environmental exposure is what accumulates inside it. The incubator card's label is environmental exposure.**

Incubator establishes environmental conditions and exposure potential.

## Product model

```text
incubator conditions × duration × recurrence × intensity × pathway = exposure dose
```

SEEN retains separate historical slices for:

1. birth incubator;
2. each incubator lived for six months or longer;
3. current incubator.

Six months is SEEN's minimum residence threshold. It is a product and modeling rule: enough sustained contact for a incubator to qualify for evaluation.

Each incubator may contribute distinct physical, social, economic, cultural, institutional, geographic, and temporal conditions. Duration changes estimated dose. Recurrence, intensity, pathway, evidence quality, and historical fit determine what the system may support.

## Canonical distinctions

- Exposure magnitude requires conditions plus time and relevant pathways.
- Environmental evidence describes an environment before it describes a person.
- A supported environmental pattern may become a conditioning input.
- Missing historical evidence remains unavailable.
