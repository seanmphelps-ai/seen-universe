Use Subject System for the person being read.

The Subject System is the living human system under interpretation: their history, adaptations, relational patterning, nervous system tendencies, environmental conditioning, behavioral evidence, and current state.

Modalities are source systems.

Markers are specific signals inside modalities.

Lenses are behavioral interpretation frames.

Passes are execution actions.

The SEEN Protocol applies approved modalities, markers, and lenses to the Subject System through ordered pass execution.

## Runtime Boundary

A pass acts on the material.

A lens interprets the material.

A modality supplies a source system.

A marker supplies a specific signal inside a modality.

## User-Facing Boundary

Use person in user-facing copy when Subject System would feel mechanical.

Use Subject System in canon, schema, runtime, and architecture language when the system must distinguish the human being read from modalities, markers, lenses, and passes.
