# GENERATOR / HELIX CONTEXT

The Generator determines what exists. The Oracle and rendering layers present it.

## Invariants

- One permanent 64-portal registry
- Separate complete modality layers
- Append first, synthesize second, render third
- No cancellation
- Evidence and provenance preserved
- Person, place, time, and modality remain independently inspectable
- Source calculations remain immutable after completion
- Creative rendering cannot add, remove, or alter supported Generator findings

## Helix

The Helix is the visible convergence model. Its strands preserve independent signals while showing convergence, divergence, contradiction, recurrence, threshold, and pressure across people, places, systems, and time.

## Life Map

The Life Map is the public visual projection of the system, not the proprietary implementation.

- Center the permanent 64-portal lattice/field.
- Preserve independent source-system strands.
- Represent Forge/environment/exposure as contextual pressure around the pattern rather than another source system.
- Represent timing, relationships, events, wounds, and recurrence as activation/pressure layers.
- Illuminate only supported convergence.
- Allow drill-down from visible convergence to preserved source records and evidence.
- Keep proprietary weights, hidden mappings, prompts, scoring internals, and server-side routing private.

## Rendering boundary

Rendering begins only after supported Generator findings exist.

- Multiple render modes may express the same immutable claim set.
- Each rendered output preserves `claimRefs` and `evidenceRefs`.
- Narrative, screenplay, documentary, storyteller, and metaphor modes may change voice, sequence, scene construction, and imagery without changing underlying findings.
- Metaphor remains labeled/traceable as presentation rather than source evidence.

Rule: `Creativity after convergence, not before.`

## Shadow chain

Input → pressure → trigger → adaptation → behavior → belief → capacity → cost → consequence → intervention → expected change → confidence.
