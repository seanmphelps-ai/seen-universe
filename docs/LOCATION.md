# LOCATION — what this product uses

This is the location we talked about. Not a second engine. Not helix.
Lived places write the card. They do not move the planets.

## Why it matters

Same person. Different place. Different training.
The chart is the cutting. The place is the pot and the weather.
Roots can take. Roots can rot. The seed is not constant.
Location and chart are equal. Neither whispers by rule.

A place is soil:
- what gets rewarded
- what gets punished
- what gets ignored
- what earns status
- what creates belonging
- what creates exclusion
- what people aspire toward
- what people are afraid of losing
- what people spend money on when they have a choice
- what the child learns is safe
- what the child learns is dangerous
- what capacity the soil builds
- what capacity the soil starves
- what it costs the body after years
- what happens if you stay
- what happens if you leave

## When it enters

1. Name
2. Birth date
3. Birth city
4. Lived places + years (six months counts)
5. Then the dark time cards

Do not put a location page before the date.
Do not score the sky with the places.
1993 in a city is not 2026 in that city.
Hills are not flats. Same city name is not the same soil.

## What it does on the first cards

Lived years go into the paragraph.
The paragraph still has to run:
trigger, pressure, behavior, collapse, thrive, cost to them, cost to others, what is lost if it runs one more cycle.

No one line.
No clock on the card.
No planet names on the card.

## Gift and cost cards

Those come after the first time-slice works.
Every location card has gift and cost together.
Never only-good. Never only-bad.
User picks the field they lived.

## Do not

- Invent a second location engine
- Call Census or BLS
- Rebuild city search
- Average two places into one person
- Write “seed stays constant”
- Write “location only whispers”
